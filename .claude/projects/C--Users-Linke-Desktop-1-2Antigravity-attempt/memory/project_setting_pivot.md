name: Setting Direction Change
description: Game setting pivoted from company/employee to TV show contestant in March 2026. Three-layer structure compressed to two layers. Orders replaced by event-driven needs system.
type: project
---

As of late March 2026, the team has abandoned the "company employee operating a machine" setting documented in setting-current-state.md.

**New direction: TV show contestant.** The player's character participates in a TV show where the game mechanic (matrix draw) IS the show. Items drawn from the show are used to meet the character's real needs outside the show.

**Structural change: three layers → two layers.**
- Old: draw items → fulfill orders → earn score/rewards
- New: draw items (these ARE what you want) → use them to handle life events outside the show

**Orders → Event system.** The left-side panel now shows narrative events (life crises, opportunities) instead of abstract orders. Events are state-driven, time-structured (3 days), and interconnected through consequences.

**Why:** The team felt the game's theme ("how humans coexist with uncertainty") wasn't landing because items and actions lacked experiential weight. The TV show + urgent character needs framing is an attempt to give the uncertainty real emotional stakes.

**How to apply:** The documents in design_docs/ (setting-current-state.md, game_rules.md, gameplay_progress.md) still reflect the OLD company setting. They need updating once the prototype validates the new direction. Until then, trust conversation context over document content for setting-related decisions.
