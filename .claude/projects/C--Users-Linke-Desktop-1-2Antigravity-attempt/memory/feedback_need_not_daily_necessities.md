---
name: Need ≠ Daily Necessities
description: Critical clarification — when the team says the character "needs" items from the show, this does NOT mean daily survival goods. "Need" is defined by the character's specific story/situation, not by generic life requirements.
type: feedback
---

The team's intent: the character draws items from the show that they genuinely "need" — but "need" is determined by WHO they are and WHAT SITUATION they're in, not by basic human survival (food, shelter, hygiene).

The repeated trap: every time we designed events, "need" defaulted to daily necessities (rice, soap, blanket, medicine), which made events feel mundane and narrow. This is wrong.

**Why:** "Need" was casually described as "生活需要" (life needs), but the actual meaning is "在节目的范畴之外需要的东西" (things needed outside the show's context). What those things ARE depends entirely on the character's specific story. A person in hiding needs different things than a person rebuilding a business than a person trying to clear their name.

**How to apply:** When designing events and items, NEVER start from "what does a person need to live?" Start from "who is this specific person, what is their specific situation, and what do THEY specifically need?" The items should be as unique as the character's story.
